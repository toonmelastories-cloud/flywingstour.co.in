The Visa Process, and Why We Are Not Quoting a Fee

Indian nationals need a visa to enter Japan, applied for through an authorised visa application centre with your passport, photographs, itinerary and financial documents. Processing is generally straightforward, but published fee figures from different sources genuinely disagree right now, some report a flat concessional rate for Indian applicants, others describe a broader fee increase affecting visa-required markets from mid-2026. Rather than quote a number that may already be wrong by the time you read this, we recommend confirming the current fee and document list directly with the Embassy of Japan in India before you apply, and we're happy to walk you through the checklist either way.

Getting There from Chandigarh

There is no direct flight from Chandigarh to Japan, so the trip routes through a connection, most commonly Delhi, with onward service to Tokyo's Narita or Haneda airport. Total travel time typically runs nine to twelve hours depending on the connection and layover. Build a buffer into your first day's plan rather than scheduling activities right after landing, since the time difference (Japan is three and a half hours ahead of India) and the long flight both take a day to adjust to.

The Classic Circuit: Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka

Most first-time visitors from India follow a version of the same route, often called the Golden Triangle, because it covers Japan's modern capital, its historic former capital, and its food capital in one trip.

  • Tokyo (3-4 days): Shibuya and Shinjuku for the city's energy, Asakusa's Senso-ji temple for a quieter, older side of the city, a day trip to Hakone or Mount Fuji's viewing points if weather allows
  • Kyoto (2-3 days): Fushimi Inari's torii gates, Kinkaku-ji's Golden Pavilion, the Arashiyama bamboo grove, and the Gion district for a chance to spot a geisha in the evening
  • Osaka (1-2 days): Dotonbori's street food and neon, Osaka Castle, and a day trip to Nara to see the famous deer park if time allows

Travel between these three cities runs on Japan's bullet train network, the Shinkansen, which is fast, punctual, and genuinely one of the highlights of the trip rather than just transport.

Should You Buy a JR Pass?

The Japan Rail Pass covers unlimited travel on most JR trains, including the Shinkansen, for a fixed number of days. It makes sense if your itinerary involves several long-distance train journeys between cities, since buying individual tickets for the same routes usually costs more. If your trip stays mostly within one city or region, individual tickets or a regional pass may work out cheaper. Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a direct flight from Chandigarh to Japan?

No, the trip connects through a hub, most commonly Delhi, with onward service to Tokyo. Total travel time is usually nine to twelve hours depending on the connection.

What is the best time to visit Japan from India?

Late March to early April for cherry blossoms, or November for autumn colours, are the two most popular windows and need early booking. Winter offers a quieter, often better-value alternative.

Do Indian citizens need a visa for Japan?

Yes, a visa is required, applied for through an authorised visa application centre. Fee figures vary across sources right now, so confirm the current amount directly with the Embassy of Japan in India.

Is Japan expensive for Indian travellers?

It is a premium destination, but a sustained weak yen has made hotels, food and shopping meaningfully better value than they were a few years ago, even though international flights remain a significant cost.

Should I buy a JR Pass?

Only if your itinerary includes several long-distance train journeys between cities. For trips that stay mostly within one region, individual tickets often cost less. We check this against your actual route.

How many days do I need for a first Japan trip?

Nine to eleven days covers the classic Tokyo-Kyoto-Osaka circuit comfortably, including a settling-in day after the long flight. Shorter trips are possible but mean choosing between the three cities rather than covering all of them.

Is Japan good for a honeymoon?

Yes, particularly during cherry blossom season or a winter onsen-town stay, and it is a genuinely different option from the usual beach honeymoon destinations.
