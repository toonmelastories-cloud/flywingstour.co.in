Why Weekday Placement Decides Everything

The single biggest variable in any long-weekend calendar is not which festivals fall in a given year, those are fixed regardless, it is which day of the week they land on. A holiday on a Tuesday or Thursday, sitting one day away from the weekend, is worth far more than the same holiday on a Sunday, because it turns a single leave-day application into a multi-day break. A holiday that lands on Saturday or Sunday outright, meanwhile, effectively vanishes into the normal weekend and delivers nothing extra. 2027 happens to be a strong year for Chandigarh specifically because several of its biggest regional festivals, Baisakhi, Lohri, and Maghi among them, land on exactly the right side of the week. That is the entire mechanic behind the ranking below.

The April Cluster, in Detail

This is the one every generic calendar site will undersell, because they list Baisakhi, Rama Navami, and Mahavir Jayanti as three separate minor entries instead of noticing they land back to back.

  • Saturday and Sunday, April 10 and 11: Your normal weekend, the calm before the run starts.
  • Wednesday, April 14: Baisakhi, the Punjab harvest festival and the anniversary of the founding of the Khalsa, a full public holiday.
  • Thursday, April 15: Rama Navami, also a public holiday.
  • Friday, April 16: A working day, and the single leave application that unlocks the whole stretch.
  • Saturday and Sunday, April 17 and 18: Weekend, no leave required.
  • Monday, April 19: Mahavir Jayanti, another public holiday, closing out the run.

That is six consecutive days off, Wednesday to Monday, for the cost of a single Friday's leave. We are flagging this early, in August 2026, specifically because a stretch this good gets requested by half your office the moment people notice it, and flight and hotel prices for late April 2027 will move accordingly as that happens. Booking early on this one is not just convenient, it is meaningfully cheaper than waiting.

January: Two Long Weekends, Thirteen Days Apart

January 2027 is unusually generous for a month that normally just means post-New-Year routine. Two separate long weekends sit less than two weeks apart.

Lohri and Maghi (January 13 to 17)

Lohri falls on Wednesday, January 13, and Maghi follows immediately on Thursday, January 14. Apply a single day of leave on Friday, January 15, and the run extends straight through the weekend to Sunday, January 17, five days off in total. This is a distinctly Punjab-flavoured long weekend that national calendars tend to compress into a single generic entry, when in practice it is two consecutive regional holidays that happen to land midweek.

Republic Day (January 23 to 26)

Republic Day itself falls on Tuesday, January 26. Take Monday, January 25, as leave and the weekend before it, Saturday and Sunday the 23rd and 24th, rolls straight into the holiday for a four-day break. Coming so soon after the Lohri-Maghi stretch, this is a good one to combine into a single longer leave block if your workplace allows it, rather than two separate short trips.

The Reliable Middle: Holi and Diwali

Two of the year's biggest festivals fall in genuinely useful spots on the calendar without needing any leave at all.

  • Holi, Monday, March 22: Paired with the preceding Saturday and Sunday for a clean three-day weekend, zero leave required. Domestic hill destinations and Rajasthan see a real spike in bookings around this window, so early booking still pays off even though the dates themselves need no leave application.
  • Diwali, Friday, October 29: A Friday holiday is about as good as Diwali's calendar placement gets, rolling straight into the weekend for three days off with zero leave needed. Is 2027 Better or Worse Than 2026 for Long Weekends

Worth a quick honest comparison since people planning ahead often ask this. 2026's long weekend calendar leaned more heavily on Diwali and the Christmas-New Year corridor, both of which fell on strong weekday placements that year. 2027 is different in shape rather than better or worse overall, it trades a slightly quieter festive-season calendar for the standout April cluster, which has no real equivalent in 2026. Planning Around Notice Periods

The math in this calendar only works if the leave actually gets approved, and the single biggest reason a good long weekend plan falls apart is applying for leave too late. A few practical notes from years of watching this play out for our clients:

  • The April cluster will be the most contested leave request of the year. If your workplace runs on a first-come basis for popular dates, apply the moment your leave calendar opens for 2027, not closer to the date.
  • Coordinate with your travel companions' leave approval before booking anything non-refundable. A flight booked before a spouse's or friend's leave is confirmed is a common and entirely avoidable source of stress.
  • Build a one-day buffer into international itineraries during the April window specifically, since it falls right at the edge of several destinations' seasonal weather shifts, and a delayed connection eating into a six-day trip hurts more than it would on a longer holiday.

A Tricity Wrinkle: Three Holiday Lists, One Calendar

Chandigarh itself is a Union Territory with its own gazetted holiday list, while Mohali sits in Punjab and Panchkula sits in Haryana, and the three do not always match exactly. Most of the dates in this guide, the national holidays and the major Sikh and Punjabi festivals, are observed consistently across all three. Where it gets inconsistent is around smaller restricted or optional holidays, regional observances that some state governments gazette and others leave as a "restricted" choice for employees to pick from a list rather than a fixed day off.

If you work for a Punjab or Haryana state government office, a bank, or a large organisation with a formal holiday circular, it is worth checking whether Lohri, Maghi, or Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti sit on your specific employer's fixed list or their restricted-holiday list, since a restricted holiday still needs to be actively chosen and applied for rather than being an automatic day off. This is a small detail, but it is exactly the kind of thing that quietly breaks a long-weekend plan for someone who assumed a date was fixed when it was actually optional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best long weekend of 2027 for travel?

The Baisakhi to Mahavir Jayanti cluster, April 14 to 19, is the standout. It delivers six consecutive days off for a single day of leave, the best ratio anywhere on the 2027 calendar.

Is Republic Day 2027 a long weekend?

Yes, with one day of leave. Republic Day falls on Tuesday, January 26, so taking Monday the 25th off connects it to the preceding weekend for four consecutive days.

Do Lohri and Maghi both count as public holidays?

In Punjab, yes, both are observed as regional public holidays, falling on Wednesday, January 13, and Thursday, January 14, in 2027 respectively.

Why does this calendar differ from generic national long-weekend lists?

Most national calendars either skip Punjab-specific festivals like Lohri, Maghi, and Baisakhi entirely or list them as single isolated dates without noticing how they cluster with national holidays like Rama Navami and Mahavir Jayanti in 2027 specifically.

Should I book flights now for an April 2027 trip?

Not this early, but you should apply for the leave now. Flight prices for domestic and international routes typically firm up meaningfully 10 to 12 weeks out, so booking in late January or early February 2027 is the practical window, while the leave application itself is worth locking in as soon as possible.

Are Lohri and Maghi guaranteed holidays for everyone in the Tricity?

Not automatically for everyone. They are standard gazetted holidays for most Punjab-based organisations, but some employers, particularly in Chandigarh's Union Territory administration or multinational offices, list them as restricted or optional holidays instead. Check your specific employer's official circular rather than assuming.

What if my employer's holiday list does not match these dates exactly?

That is expected and common, since Chandigarh, Punjab, and Haryana each issue their own gazetted holiday notifications, and private employers often follow a modified version of one of these lists. Treat this guide as the national and Punjab-regional baseline, then cross-check the specific dates against your own workplace circular once it is released, typically toward the end of the preceding year.
