Quick context: Indian tourists no longer need the old Inner Line Permit document for general Leh sightseeing. An online Ladakh environment fee through the LAHDC Leh portal covers that now. A genuine Inner Line Permit is still required for Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley, Tso Moriri and Hanle, the border zones most people come to Ladakh to see.

The practical takeaway: the "no permit needed" headline only covers basic Leh sightseeing. The moment your plan includes Pangong Tso or Nubra Valley, which is almost every plan, you still need the ILP. Rules here change often, so confirm them with your operator before you travel.

Acclimatisation Decides the Trip

Leh sits at about 3,500 metres. Khardung La is above 5,300 metres, Pangong Tso around 4,300 and Tso Moriri near 4,500. Altitude sickness is a genuine medical risk here, and it should shape your itinerary more than any other factor.

  • Rest for the first 24 to 48 hours in Leh, with nothing more than short walks, before any higher excursion.
  • Skip alcohol and heavy exertion for two days, since both slow your body's adjustment.
  • Drink more water than feels necessary, because dehydration makes every symptom worse.
  • Descend at once if warning signs appear: severe headache, persistent nausea or breathlessness at rest.
  • Check with a doctor first if you have a heart or respiratory condition, and tell your operator so the plan can build in extra buffer.

More Ladakh trips are cut short by altitude sickness than by any logistics failure, and nearly all of them trace back to an itinerary that skipped this rest window.

Chandigarh to Leh: Road Trip or Flight

The Manali-Leh Road Trip

You drive up to Manali first, then roughly 490 kilometres over some of the highest motorable passes in the world. The road gains height gradually over two to three days, which genuinely helps acclimatisation, but it uses days a flight would save, and it is usually open only from around June to September. A third route runs from Srinagar through Kargil, which pairs well with a Kashmir stay.

Flying to Leh

Faster, but your body gets no gradual adjustment at all: you go from near sea level to 3,500 metres in under two hours, which makes the rest days above more important, not less. Most departures from Chandigarh connect through Delhi, and our flight booking desk can check which option suits your dates.

Our honest read: with ten or more days, drive up one way and fly back. With a week or less, fly both ways and protect the rest schedule rather than compressing it.

What a Leh Ladakh Package from Chandigarh Covers

Leh Town and Monasteries, 1 to 2 Days

Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace and the monasteries at Thiksey and Hemis. Only the environment fee is needed here, no ILP.

Nubra Valley, 1 to 2 Days

Reached over Khardung La, with sand dunes and double-humped Bactrian camels set against the Himalaya. Requires the ILP.

Pangong Tso, 1 to 2 Days

The turquoise lake behind most people's picture of Ladakh. Stay overnight rather than rushing a day trip, because sunrise before the Leh crowds arrive is the reason to go. Requires the ILP.

Tso Moriri, Optional

Quieter than Pangong with a similar lake landscape, worth adding if you have spare days. Requires the ILP.

A Sample 8-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Leh by air and rest completely, with a short evening walk only
  • Day 2: Continued acclimatisation, gentle town sightseeing and Shanti Stupa
  • Day 3: Leh Palace, Thiksey and Hemis monasteries
  • Day 4: Drive to Nubra Valley over Khardung La, overnight in Nubra
  • Day 5: Sand dunes, a camel ride and Diskit Monastery
  • Day 6: Drive to Pangong Tso, overnight at the lake
  • Day 7: Sunrise at Pangong, drive back to Leh
  • Day 8: Departure

Add two or three days for Tso Moriri or a Manali road leg.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indian tourists still need a permit for Ladakh in 2026?

Not for general Leh sightseeing, which needs only the online Ladakh environment fee through the LAHDC Leh portal. A genuine Inner Line Permit is still required for Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley, Tso Moriri and Hanle.

Is there a direct flight from Chandigarh to Leh?

Most departures connect through Delhi, where IndiGo and Air India fly the Delhi to Leh sector. Direct Chandigarh to Leh services have run seasonally, so check your specific dates.

How many days does a Ladakh trip need?

Seven to eight days covers Leh, Nubra Valley and Pangong Tso while respecting the rest schedule. Ten or more days allows a one-way Manali road trip or a Tso Moriri extension.

Is altitude sickness a serious risk in Ladakh?

Yes. Leh sits at about 3,500 metres and several key stops sit higher. Resting for 24 to 48 hours on arrival is the single most important thing a first-time visitor can do.

Can families with young children do a Ladakh trip?

Yes, with care: see a paediatrician first and build in extra rest days.
